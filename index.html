About

DeWayne Searcy, PhD, is Chief Operating Officer of Cowin Equipment Company — a Southeast-based heavy-equipment dealer headquartered in Birmingham, Alabama. Previously an accounting professor and Director at Auburn University’s School of Accountancy, he works at the intersection of data analysis, operational process, financial frameworks, and the behavioral drivers of financial performance.

12toKNOW is where that work — and the projects it spills into — gets published in the open: field-tested, rigor-first, and free to use.
