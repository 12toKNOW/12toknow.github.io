What it found the results

  • The family line traced back to Bern, 1387 (the de Graffenried patriline) and Derbyshire, c.1490 (the Needham line) — with ~79% of ancestors documented to a primary source.
  • DNA integrated on both sides: Y-DNA I-A16735 genetically confirmed the Searcy male line (via a documented match), and mtDNA J1c4k traced the maternal line — 31 Big Y matches in all.
  • Four Revolutionary-War Patriot pathways established for DAR lineage.
  • Landmark findings: the fabricated "Sir Arthur Needham" disproved and the true parents restored; the Three Aarons resolved from a pension register in the ancestor's own father's hand; the Swiss patriline documented to 1387; and the northeast-Mississippi family convergence mapped.
