A Working Literature Review

A Thematic Bibliography

A reading map of the field — respected, academically focused scholarship on Russian history, grouped by subject. Weighted toward university presses and peer-reviewed venues.

This is a reading map rather than an argument: the scholarship a serious student of Russian history is expected to know. It is deliberately generous — in a literature review, an extra title is a smaller sin than a missing one — and it complements the finished, synopsized Reading. Inclusion signals that the field engages a work, not that every claim in it has held up.

Principal journals for the field: Slavic Review · The Russian Review · Kritika: Explorations in Russian and Eurasian History · Jahrbücher für Geschichte Osteuropas · Europe-Asia Studies · Cahiers du monde russe · Revolutionary Russia · Journal of Cold War Studies.

Adapted from Russia & Siberia — A Reader's Companion, Part XIII. Part of the Russian History guide.
