The Searcy Family History

Stories from the Record

The best discoveries, told as stories — a carpenter who built for Henry VIII, a knight in the Queen's guard, an ancestor who never existed, a line traced to a treasurer of Bern, four Patriots of the Revolution, and the three Aarons untangled by a single pension file. Each one begins with a document and ends with a person.

Behind every name in the register is a paper trail, and behind a few of them is a genuine mystery — a false ancestor, a corrected claim, a tangle of same-named men, a house founded on a king's grant, a line that reached impossibly far back. These are those, told roughly in the order the family lived them, from a Tudor priory to a Mississippi ridge.
