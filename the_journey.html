The Searcy Family History

The Journey

Five centuries of a family, drawn as one long move — out of the Old World, across the Atlantic, and down through the American South to the corner of the map where every line finally meets.

Two old-world lines — English gentry and Swiss patricians — cross an ocean and travel two thousand miles of frontier to converge on one Alabama city.

The main line (Searcy · Rorie · Hubbard) The maternal lines (Daniel · Mitchem · Brown) The Atlantic crossing, 1714

Positions follow real geography but the map is lightly schematic, drawn for the story rather than to exact scale.
