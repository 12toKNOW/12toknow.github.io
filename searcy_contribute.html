Add to the Family Record

Have a name, a story, an old photo, a document — or a correction? Send it in. Every submission is reviewed, and the good ones become part of the tree.

This is a living record, and the best of it comes from family. Share what you have and I’ll fold verified pieces into the history — with a source note, the way everything here is done.

A note on privacy: nothing is published automatically. Everything is reviewed first, living relatives are always kept private, and sensitive documents are never posted publicly — only the facts and the source note make it onto the site.
