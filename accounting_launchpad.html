Accounting as a launchpad.

An audience-first body of work on the accounting career — majoring, early paths, the credentialing threshold, and the pivots that follow. The spine of it: accounting isn't a destination, it's a launchpad to operating leadership.

Most advice about accounting stops at the first job. This series takes the longer view — from why the major is worth choosing, through the credential that opens the door, to the pivots that turn a technical foundation into a seat at the table. It's the throughline of my own non-linear path: professor and director, then operating executive. The pieces below are being written and added over time; four are live now.
